## Acoustics: what the ISO 23351-1 classes mean

The number that matters for an acoustic phone booth is its **speech level reduction to ISO 23351-1** in dB(A): how much quieter speech is outside than inside. The classes:

| Class | Speech level reduction | In practice |
|---|---|---|
| A+ | ≥ 33 dB | not audible outside the booth |
| A | ≥ 30 dB | barely audible |
| B | ≥ 27 dB | a muffled murmur, not intelligible |
| C | ≥ 24 dB | a distant murmur, not intelligible |
| D | ≥ 21 dB | audible, fragments intelligible |

**Solo Flex achieves about 24 dB(A), Class C; Modular XL 25.9 dB(A), also Class C.** For calls and video meetings in an open-plan office that solves the problem: colleagues hear a distant murmur but no words. For confidential meetings right next to a busy floor, choose a higher class or a closed room, and check the exact requirement with your acoustician.

Ask every supplier for the ISO 23351-1 test report.

## Ventilation, light and power

After acoustics, ventilation decides whether a booth gets used: a stuffy booth ends up with its door open. Every Re-Sound booth is ventilated; the electrical models use a silent EC-fan system.

- **Air change**: Solo Flex encloses at most 1.02 × 1.02 × 2.26 = 2.35 m³ (outer dimensions); at 4.6 m³/min that is replaced every 2.35 / 4.6 = 0.51 minutes, roughly every 30 seconds.
- **Control**: one PIR sensor switches fans and LED lighting when you step in.
- **Power**: 230 V and USB-C inside, so nothing trails through the door.

## Booth or panels? A worked example

A phone booth solves one person talking; it does not shorten the reverberation of the whole floor, which is a job for acoustic panels. ISO 22955 (acoustic quality of open-plan offices) and ISO 3382-3 (how speech decays over distance) set the direction, and BS 8233 is the British guidance on sound insulation and noise reduction for buildings; check the exact requirement with your acoustician.

Example: an open-plan office of 240 m² × 3 m = 720 m³, measured reverberation time 1.2 s, target 0.7 s. Sabine's formula A = 0.161 × V / T:

- A today = 0.161 × 720 / 1.2 = **96.6 m² Sabine**
- A target = 0.161 × 720 / 0.7 = **165.6 m² Sabine**
- To add: 165.6 − 96.6 = **69.0 m² Sabine**

With [rPET acoustic panels](/en/products/pet-acoustic-panels) in absorption class B (αw 0.85 to ISO 11654) that is 69.0 / 0.85 = **81 m² of panels** on ceiling and walls, a first estimate; check the exact requirement with your acoustician. The booth comes on top, not instead.
