## D. Do you want panels orderable online at all?

Booths are one price times a quantity, which is why they went online first.
Panels are sold per square metre, so ordering them online needs the buyer to
enter a surface area or a panel count, pick a thickness and a finish, and hit
a minimum order. That is a bigger piece of work than filling in a price.

Answer one of:

- **Price on the page only** — the price appears on the product page, in the
  range tables and in the Google structured data, and the buyer still requests
  a quote. Fast, and worth doing on its own for search.
- **Price and online ordering** — as above, plus a configurator per panel
  family. Needs the answers in C1 to C3 including the thickness and finish
  questions.

Prices are useful for search either way: a page carrying a real price is
eligible for the price shown in Google results; "On request" is not.

## Where these live in the code

For whoever applies the answers:

- Product prices: `src/data/products.ts` (`fromPrice`, `priceUnit`) **and**
  `src/lib/order/catalogue.ts` (`unitPriceExclVat`). The two are checked
  against each other, so both change together or the product stops being
  orderable.
- Option and add-on prices: `src/lib/order/catalogue.ts`, the `options` array
  of each entry. `priceExclVat: null` is what renders as "On request";
  `perUnit` is the per-booth / per-item switch from block B.
- Add-on labels: `messages/<locale>.json`, under
  `soloFlexPage.addons`, `duoPage.addons`, `modularXlPage.addons`.

Adding a price is a one-line change per item. No translation is needed —
amounts are formatted per locale automatically.
